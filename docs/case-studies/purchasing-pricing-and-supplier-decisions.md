# Purchasing, Pricing & Supplier Decisions

**Context:** Specialty instrument repair business, 2022–2025  
**Focus:** Supplier comparisons, purchasing choices, pricing analysis, invoice visibility and supply continuity

## The commercial problem

A low listed price is not automatically the best purchase. Supplier discounts, available stock, quantities, lead times and the price quoted to a customer all affect whether an order makes sense for a working repair shop.

I needed usable information for decisions about what to purchase, from whom, when to reorder and how to price customer work.

## What I actually did

- **Built and used Excel decision tools.** I created supplier pricing, purchasing, inventory and profitability workbooks for actual small-business purchasing and pricing decisions.
- **Analyzed supplier economics.** I compared costs, supplier discounts and order quantities instead of relying on the first unit price shown.
- **Connected demand with purchasing.** I used historical sales information to examine buying patterns and adjust purchasing decisions as the business developed.
- **Managed order and invoice visibility.** I built and used a supplier-account tracker with order/invoice identifiers, due-date formulas and outstanding-balance visibility to support purchasing and payables planning.
- **Executed purchasing decisions.** My broader firsthand commercial work also includes preparing purchase orders and routing contractual documents for signatures. That is operational execution, not a claim of enterprise procurement-department or legal approval authority.
- **Connected purchases to customer commitments.** Parts availability and supplier pricing affected service estimates, scheduling and what the shop could responsibly promise.

## The supplier-continuity example

I identified a critical repair part that created **single-source supplier dependence**. I investigated **five alternatives**, obtained approval for a suitable alternative, made a purchase and identified an additional backup source.

**Result:** A concrete alternate purchasing path and backup were established. I am not claiming an independently measured cost reduction, stockout prevention rate or audited margin gain.

## Evidence and business scale

Original spreadsheets and selected vendor invoices have been reviewed privately and cross-matched in the career-evidence work. Those documents are not published. My use of the tools and the supplier-continuity decision are also supported by firsthand accounts, not by a certified corporate procurement history.

This is credible experience in **purchasing execution, supplier evaluation, Excel analysis, pricing decisions, commercial recordkeeping and risk-aware sourcing**.
