# Purchasing, Pricing & Supplier Decisions

**Context:** Owner-operated specialty instrument repair business, 2022–2025  
**Focus:** Supplier comparisons, purchasing decisions, invoice visibility and supply continuity

## The business problem

In a repair business, the purchase price of a part is only one part of the decision. Availability, supplier terms, discounts, stock already on hand and the price quoted to a customer all affect what the business can reasonably do. I needed usable information for those decisions without turning every purchase into a separate research exercise.

## What I did

- **Built and used Excel purchasing and pricing tools.** I organized supplier pricing, inventory and profitability information into workbooks I could consult when deciding what and how much to buy.
- **Compared costs and supplier options.** I used supplier price information and discount structures when evaluating orders, rather than treating the lowest listed unit price as the entire decision.
- **Tracked purchasing obligations.** I created a supplier-account workbook with order and invoice references, due-date formulas and outstanding-balance visibility to help plan upcoming purchases and payables.
- **Connected purchasing with service work.** Parts availability and pricing informed repair estimates, customer commitments and the order of work in the shop.

## One concrete supplier-continuity decision

I identified a critical repair part with single-source exposure. I researched **five possible alternatives**, obtained approval for an alternative, made a purchase from that supplier and established another backup source for future needs.

The action was to reduce dependence on one supplier. I am not claiming an independently measured savings figure or a guaranteed period without stockouts.

## What this work demonstrates

This was direct purchasing and pricing work in a **small, owner-operated business**, not a claim of managing an enterprise procurement department. It demonstrates practical supplier evaluation, cost analysis, recordkeeping, inventory judgment and following a sourcing decision through to an actual purchase.

My original spreadsheets and selected supplier records have been reviewed privately to corroborate parts of this work. Those records contain business information and are **not reproduced here**. The sourcing example and business use of the tools are described from my firsthand experience; no audited financial improvement is asserted.
