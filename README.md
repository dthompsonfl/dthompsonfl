## How I approach problems

**Operating perspective:** Who uses this? What decision must they make? Where does the process fail, and what information do they need to recover?

**Engineering perspective:** Where is authoritative state? What happens on duplicate requests or partial failure? Are permissions enforced at the right boundary? What can be tested, and what still needs real-world validation?

Working across both sides helps me translate practical requirements into implementation choices that operators, customers and developers can understand.
